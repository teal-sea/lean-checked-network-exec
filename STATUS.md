done and no evidence exists.

## Current: proved general integer transformer engine (E4 to E8)

- **[E4](E4-REPORT.md):** Lean 4.35.0 (`leanchecker --fresh`) and `nanoda_bin` 0.4.19 (9,261 declarations, 0 typechecker errors) checked exact logits and next-token predictions for the translated safe Rust engine (`LcnExec.GeneralEngine.general_engine_correct`, `forward_logits_spec`; standard axioms `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound` only). Fresh Charon/Aeneas extraction matched byte for byte, and three isolated Rust mutations (`shift`, `alpha`, `tie`) were rejected after re-extraction.
- **[E5](E5-REPORT.md):** Trained two-layer byte-level LM (`weights/tiny_bytelm_v1.bin`, 464,128 params, 1.3866 bits/byte) and mod-113 weights (`weights/mod113_v1.bin`, 12,769 / 12,769 exact, logit hash `6cb24f7670e8562e`) passed `load_check` and matched the exact Python reference across 51,200 tiny-LM logits and 22,600 mod-113 logits with 0 mismatches.
- **[E6](E6-REPORT.md):** Compiled the exact proved `rust/general_engine` crate to WebAssembly (29,755 bytes, Rust 1.99.1, overflow checks on). Chromium 156 matched native Rust on all 256 logits and 5 greedy prompts, rejected a 1-byte corrupt weight upload and restored generation cleanly, and verified all 12,769 / 12,769 mod-113 pairs in 66.70 seconds (the original under-1-second stretch target was not met; no unchecked or lookup shortcut was substituted).
- **E7 / E8:** Audit surface, trusted tool versions, scope limits, and MIT license consolidated in [README.md](README.md); published 2026-11-25.
